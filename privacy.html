Privacy Policy

Last updated August 1, 2026

ARG Home Improvements & Remodeling, Inc. respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the website collects and how it is used when you request a consultation or use the renovation concept tool.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, project ZIP code, project details, preferred timing, estimated investment range, notes, and photographs you choose to upload.

Renovation concept images

Photos submitted to the renovation concept tool are processed to create the requested visual concept. Do not upload photographs containing sensitive personal documents, people who have not consented, or information unrelated to the project.

How information is used

  • To prepare renovation concepts and preliminary estimates.
  • To respond to inquiries and discuss potential projects.
  • To maintain lead and project-planning records.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.

Service providers

Information may be processed by hosting, storage, spreadsheet, and artificial-intelligence service providers solely to operate the requested website features and manage project inquiries.

Preliminary estimates

Online estimates and concept images are planning tools. Final scope, feasibility, materials, schedule, permitting, and pricing require professional review and a written agreement.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information submitted through the website, subject to legal and business recordkeeping requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, call or text 631-579-3122. ARG serves Nassau and Suffolk County from North Babylon, New York.
